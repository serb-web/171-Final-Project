About Me

Hi! I am Bradley, currently a student in the University of the Philippines Open University studying Associate of Arts in Digital Design and Arts. My personal interests often revolve around interactive entertainment, notably the possibilities on how it can allow the audience to participate, make choices, and influence outcomes based on the multimodal nature of multimedia in ways that are an extension of my creative imagination.
